# --- prefix sum + one more macro-op: does an op that needs no ALU (nop, eliminated move, vector op) cost as much as an ALU op?

q_ps_ld: add edx, dword ptr [rdi+rax*4]; mov dword ptr [rsi+rax*4], edx; mov r11d, dword ptr [rdi+2048]; inc rax; cmp rcx, rax; jne .L
